# Company feeder kits — G1 Scope

The company's standard BOM structures, as kits with indicative prices:
- **DOL Motors BOM** (`OWN-PROPEL-DOL`): 28 DOL, forward/reverse and star-delta feeder kits for belt
  conveyors, screen / pan / grizzly / wash feeders and dewatering.
- **Standard panel costing sheet** (`OWN-PROPEL-COST`): 22 panel kits, among them star-delta panels
  55–315 kW with and without PLC.

Each kit is checked against the motor:
- MPCB range against the motor current;
- contactor rating;

- the requirement flow.
