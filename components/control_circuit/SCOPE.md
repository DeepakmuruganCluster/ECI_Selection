# Control circuit — G1 Scope

The 230 V control supply:
- the control load;
- a company-standard control transformer (or phase-neutral);
- its primary and secondary MCBs.

Plus the operator devices from the company costing sheet:
- emergency stop + hood;
- reset push button;
- phase lamps;
- hour meter.

Device consumption (coil / lamp VA) is asked; it is not in the held data.
