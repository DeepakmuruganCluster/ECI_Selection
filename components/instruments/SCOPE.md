# Field devices — G1 Scope

The panel-side needs of the client's field devices (instruments, switches, valves, pneumatic panels, tower lamps):
- 230 V MCBs for instruments and valves;
- fused terminals for analog loops;
- the 24 V DC load;
- checks of the client's own I/O arithmetic and tag ranges.

Not yet: surge protection for field signals and the 24 V DC field supply (not in the held data).
