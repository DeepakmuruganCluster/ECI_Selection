# Incomer MCCB (3VA1, TM240) — G1 Scope

**What it decides:** the Siemens SENTRON 3VA1 moulded-case circuit breaker for line protection with the TM240 ATAM
trip unit (adjustable thermal, adjustable magnetic): article number, frame, rated current In, breaking-capacity
class with Icu / Ics at 380 … 415 V, and — when its data sheet is held — the Ir / Ii ranges and the Ir setting.

**Why:** owner 2026-10-20, RFQ part 1 — the client (ECI-WNS2503-AUTO p.1) asks "3VA-630A 55KA Adjustable Thermal
Magnetic" for both panel incomers. The 3VM component (components/mccb) stays as it is.

**Sources:** Siemens Catalog LV 10 · 2026 (SIE-LV10-2026) PDF pp.181, 183, 200, 201; data sheets of single articles
(SIE-3VA1563-DS; more as saved).

**In scope:** 3VA11 … 3VA15, TM240 ATAM, 3-pole, without neutral protection, nut keeper kit, 380 … 415 V AC,
ambient ≤ 50 °C (above: only with the article's data-sheet derating).

**Out of scope (refused):** 4-pole (neutral-protection codes), TM210 / TM220 / TM120M, 3VA2 electronic trip units,
box terminals, other voltages, DC, IT networks, above 1000 A or 110 kA.
