# I/O list — G1 Scope

The PLC signals each motor feeder needs, one row per signal, for every installed unit. It covers:
- DOL starters;
- soft starters;
- VFDs on a fieldbus or hardwired;
- STO safety signals.

When the client's available channels are given, it checks that the motor signals fit.

Not in scope:
- Instrument signals (transmitters, switches, valves): they are not in the load list.
- Heater feeders (owner, 2026-10-11).
- PLC card selection: that is component 10, which is blocked on documents.

Authority: RULE. The signal standard is ours and needs the owner's sign-off (IO-1 … IO-3). No
catalogue data is used.
